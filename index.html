Simone Ciciliano

PhD Student at the Physical Computing Lab, Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, supervised by Rosella Gennari and Maristella Matera (HINT Lab, Politecnico di Milano).
I study natural language programming, with a focus on conceptual alignment
between humans and machines.
My research combines insights and methods from Natural Language Processing,
End-User Development, and Interaction Design.

Before this, I received an MA in Applied Linguistics at the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, with a thesis supervised by Rosella Gennari. And before, I collaborated with the SapienzaNLP group, and completed an internship at the MaiNLP Lab.

I am responsible for the high-performence machines at the Physical Computing Lab.

In languages with grammatical gender and/or gender agreement, you can refer to me with masculine forms.

Research lines

Some topics I am currently working on:

  • Conceptual Alignment: How concepts are represented in humans and machines, and the one can inform the other.
  • Natural Language Programming: How executable information is conveyed in natural language.
  • Tangible and Embodied Interaction: How natural language conveys executable information for different interaction modalities.
